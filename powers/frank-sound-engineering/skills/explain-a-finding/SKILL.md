# Explain a finding

Say what the person would hear, then what Frank could do, in one or two sentences each.

| Measured | Say | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| chorus lift under 1.5 LU | "The chorus doesn't feel bigger than the verse yet." | LUFS, dB, gain staging |
| vocal buried | "Your voice is a bit hidden behind the music." | EQ, masking, mids |
| clipping share | "It crackles in a few loud places." | clipping, overs, true peak |
| timing offset | "Your voice comes in a touch late against the beat." | ms, latency, quantise |
| cents off | "A few notes sit a little under where they want to be." | cents, pitch correction |
| brightness high | "It sounds a bit sharp, like it needs softening." | high end, kHz, de-ess |

Translate a number only when the person asks. Offer one thing to try, never a list of technical options.
