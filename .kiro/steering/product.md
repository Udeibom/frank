# Frank — Product Identity

## What Frank Is

Frank is a web-based music production platform for people who have music inside them but no music knowledge, no instruments, and no money for a producer. It is not a music generator. It is not a DAW. It is a collaborative production environment where the user and the system work together, iteratively, until the song in the user's head is a real, finished song.

The name is short for Frankenstein — stitched together from many powerful technologies, but feeling like one living thing to the user.

**Tagline: "Use Frank."**

## The Core Principle

Nothing in Frank is a one-off handoff. Every element the system produces is a starting point for a conversation, not a finished answer. The user can always go back, try again, compare, adjust, reject, and rebuild. This is what real music production feels like. Frank just removes the prerequisite of knowing music to do it.

## Who Frank Is For

The primary user:
- Has a voice
- Has song ideas — lyrics, melodies, feelings — that they cannot get out
- Has zero music theory knowledge
- Cannot play any instrument
- Cannot afford a producer
- Has tried tools like Suno/Udio and found the output too far from what was in their head
- Has tried DAWs and found them impenetrable

Frank does not teach music. Frank does not require music knowledge. Frank treats the user as an artist — because they are one.

## The Two Production Paths

Both paths are first-class. Neither is a simplified version of the other.

**Path A — Voice First**
The user starts with lyrics, a melody they can hum or sing, a feeling.
`Idea → Lyrics → Feel Discovery → Vocal Recording → Beat Built Around Vocal → Mix → Export`

**Path B — Beat First**
The user starts with a rhythm, a groove, a vibe.
`Feel Discovery → Beat Construction → Lyrics → Vocal Recording → Mix → Export`

Both paths are non-linear. The user can move between layers at any time. The song is always live.

## What Frank Is Not

- Not a music generator (no "generate song" button that produces a finished track)
- Not a DAW (no visible faders, no timeline unless the user understands it, no technical terms)
- Not a karaoke machine
- Not a tool that does one thing and calls it done
- Not something that sounds AI-generated

## The Emotional Bar

A person with no music knowledge sits down with Frank, starts from an idea, and leaves with a real song that sounds like what was in their head — with their voice, their words, their feeling — in under two hours. Everything in this product is in service of that outcome.
